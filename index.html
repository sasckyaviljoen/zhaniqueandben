Our story

It all began at the gym

Some love stories begin with a chance meeting, and ours began somewhere between the weights and workouts of a gym. It was a place where we both showed up to work hard, push our limits, and become better versions of ourselves. Little did we know that one ordinary day would lead to something extraordinary. Ben may have been shaking nervously when he first approached Zhanique, but what followed was worth every nervous step. Beyond the workouts, we discovered something special: a shared mindset, a determination to work hard for the things we wanted, and a belief that the best things in life are worth the effort.

As we got to know each other, our individual journeys began to merge into something greater. We found joy in the little things, from our dates and adventures to the quiet moments spent together. We learned that love is not simply about finding someone who makes you happy, but finding someone who understands your drive, supports your ambitions, and chooses to stand beside you through every challenge. We had each been working towards our own goals, but somewhere along the way, our dreams began to align. What was once about becoming the best versions of ourselves individually became about building something bigger together.

Now, as we prepare to begin this next chapter, we do so with the same determination that brought us together, only with a much greater purpose. We are no longer working towards our dreams separately; we are building a future, a home, and a life that reflects everything we believe in. There will be new goals to chase, adventures to share, challenges to overcome, and countless memories still waiting to be made. And through it all, we will keep doing what brought us here in the first place: showing up, putting in the work, and choosing each other every single day.

We may have met while working on ourselves, but we fell in love with the possibility of everything we could become together.

Please don't be late

The ceremony starts at 4pm sharp at NG Kerk Durbanville. Please arrive by 3:30pm so you're settled and seated before we begin.

No phones during the ceremony

We're having an unplugged ceremony. Please keep phones and cameras away while we say our vows. Snap away as much as you like afterwards.

Adults only

We love your little ones, but our celebration is adults only. The Birdcage seats a small party, so we can only host the guests named on your invitation. Thank you for understanding.

Enjoy a welcome drink and snacks while you wait for the newlyweds to arrive. Dinner is a set menu with a limited amount of wine on each table. For anything else, the Birdcage has a cash bar open all evening.
